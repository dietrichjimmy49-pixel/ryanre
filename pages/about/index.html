The Story

More Than a Leading Man

Ryan Rodney Reynolds was born on October 23, 1976, in Vancouver, Canada. From a young age, he showed an interest in acting and storytelling, landing his first professional television role as a teenager.

In the decades since, through determination, humor, and a willingness to take on a wide range of roles, Ryan built a career spanning television, film, and business.

"I don't expect success. I prepare for it."

Ryan Reynolds
