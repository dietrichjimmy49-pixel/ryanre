Career Overview

The Evolution of a Storyteller

Ryan Reynolds' career reflects three decades of versatility, resilience, and reinvention. From his early days on Canadian television to becoming one of the industry's most recognizable comedic and action leads, Ryan has built a body of work that spans genres, formats, and global audiences. Known for his wit, charm, and sharp storytelling instincts, he continues to push creative boundaries both in front of and behind the camera.

The Journey

Filmography Timeline

  1. 1991–1997

    Early Beginnings

    Starting out on the Canadian teen drama Hillside (aired in the U.S. as Fifteen), Ryan's early television roles laid the foundation for a career in entertainment.

  2. 1998–2005

    Building Experience

    A lead role on the sitcom Two Guys and a Girl gave way to breakout film roles in Van Wilder, Blade: Trinity, and The Amityville Horror, showcasing his range across comedy and genre film.

  3. 2005–2010

    Breakthrough Roles

    The Proposal became a box-office hit, while the tense, one-location thriller Buried earned some of the strongest reviews of his career, together proving his range.

  4. 2011–2015

    Expanding Horizons

    A commercially mixed period spanning the big-budget Green Lantern and the comedy The Change-Up, which nonetheless expanded his range and set the stage for the reinvention to come.

  5. 2016–Present

    Global Superstar

    Deadpool became a record-breaking hit for an R-rated film, launching a run of blockbusters, entrepreneurial ventures, and a distinct comedic brand that reshaped his career.
